MixyFrames Privacy Policy

Effective 10 November 2026

MixyFrames is an Android collage maker published by Xalies. It edits and exports your photos and videos on your device.

Photos and videos you select

MixyFrames uses Android’s media picker to access the photos and videos you choose. The app reads the selected media, including existing video audio and details such as dimensions, orientation and duration, to preview, edit and export your collage. Android can retain read access to selected files so you can reopen drafts.

Your picker may offer files from a cloud photo service you use. That service handles retrieving the originals under its own privacy policy.

Projects, storage and deletion

MixyFrames keeps drafts, project names, editing choices, references to selected media, preview thumbnails, layouts and templates in Android’s app-private storage. It uses temporary local files to render some exports. The app does not upload your media or projects to Xalies.

You can delete drafts in MixyFrames. To remove all local MixyFrames data, clear its storage in Android Settings or uninstall the app. Original photos and videos and collages you saved to your gallery are managed separately in your gallery or file manager.

MixyFrames disables Android app-data backup and excludes its private files from Android’s device-transfer backup. Earlier test builds allowed Android backup. Your gallery app or cloud photo service may separately back up media and exported collages according to your settings.

Exporting and sharing

When you export a collage, MixyFrames saves it to your device’s shared media library. If you choose to share it, Android grants the receiving app access to that export. The app or service you choose handles the file under its own privacy policy.

Accounts, ads and analytics

MixyFrames has no app account, advertising or analytics and does not send crash reports itself. Android and Google Play may handle installation information and diagnostics according to their policies and your device settings.

Visiting this page

This policy page does not use cookies or tracking scripts of its own. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process information such as your IP address to operate and secure the service. GitHub’s privacy statement

Contact and privacy requests

If you email Xalies, your email address and any details or attachments you provide are used to respond to your request. The email provider processes your message for delivery and storage. Correspondence is kept as needed to resolve the request or meet legal obligations.

For privacy questions or to request deletion of information you sent to Xalies, email xalies1983@gmail.com.
