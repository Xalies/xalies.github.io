Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 15, 2026

MeshVault Hub (Desktop Application)

MeshVault Hub is a desktop application that manages 3D model files on your computer. We do not collect personal information or telemetry by default.

Data We Store Locally

  • File Metadata: Title, author, tags, source URL, and thumbnails for models you add.
  • App Settings: Your preferences and library configuration.
  • License Status: Verification data if applicable.

Network Access

The app may download thumbnails or fetch model resources when you provide a source URL. If you use the MeshVault browser extension, the app accepts local uploads from it on your machine (localhost).

Data Sharing

We do not sell or share your data. No data is sent to third-party analytics services.

MeshVault Connector (Browser Extension)

The MeshVault Connector extension collects only the minimum data needed to capture supported 3D model downloads and send them to the MeshVault desktop app running on your computer.

What We Access

  • Website Content: Page titles, author names, tags, and thumbnail URLs on supported sites to build metadata.
  • Download Events: URLs needed to capture the files you choose to download.

What We Do NOT Collect

We do not track keystrokes, mouse position, or form data. We do not collect browsing history beyond the specific supported pages you interact with.

Where Data Goes

Captured files and metadata are sent only to the MeshVault desktop app on the same machine (localhost). No data is sent to external servers.
