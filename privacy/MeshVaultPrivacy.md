# MeshVault Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 20 July 2026**

MeshVault is a local-first application for managing 3D model libraries. MeshVault does not automatically collect analytics, telemetry, advertising data, browsing history or information about the contents of your model library.

## Information Stored Locally

MeshVault may store the following information on your device or within your selected MeshVault library:

- Model files, archives, images and thumbnails
- Titles, authors, descriptions, tags and source links
- Folder and collection information
- Application preferences and library configuration
- Locally generated diagnostic logs when diagnostic mode is enabled
- Trial, purchase or licence status

This information is not automatically uploaded to MeshVault.

## Support and Feedback

MeshVault includes an optional support and feedback feature. Nothing is submitted unless you choose to send a report.

A report may include:

- The report category, title and message you enter
- A contact email address, if you provide one
- The MeshVault version, build and distribution channel
- The date and time of submission
- Optional diagnostic information, when you choose to include it

Diagnostic information may include operating-system version, processor architecture, application runtime information, packaging information and a device or computer name. MeshVault does not intentionally attach your model files, library database, model metadata or images to a feedback report.

Feedback reports are processed using Google Firebase and Cloud Firestore. Google may process technical information necessary to receive the request under its own privacy terms. MeshVault does not sell feedback information or use it for advertising or tracking.

Feedback information is used only to respond to support requests, investigate problems and improve MeshVault. Reports are retained only while reasonably necessary for those purposes and may be retained for up to 24 months unless a longer period is required to resolve an ongoing issue or comply with legal obligations.

## MeshVault Connect Browser Extension

MeshVault Connect operates on supported model-repository pages when you choose to download a model. It may access the selected download URL and available page information such as the model title, author, description, tags, source link and images.

That information is sent to the MeshVault application or MeshVault Server selected by you. It is not sent to MeshVault’s developer for analytics or advertising.

MeshVault Connect does not track general browsing history, keystrokes or unrelated form information.

## MeshVault Server

If you configure MeshVault to connect to a MeshVault Server, information needed to browse or modify the remote library is sent to that server. The server location and operator are selected by you. MeshVault does not automatically relay this information to a developer-operated cloud service.

## Purchases and Licensing

App Store purchases and trial entitlements are processed by Apple through StoreKit. MeshVault receives the entitlement information needed to determine whether features are unlocked but does not receive your complete payment-card details.

Direct-build licence activation may send the licence key and activation information to Lemon Squeezy for validation. Lemon Squeezy processes that information under its own privacy policy.

## Data Sharing

MeshVault does not sell personal information, serve advertisements or track users across applications or websites.

Information is shared only:

- When you deliberately submit support or feedback
- With services needed to process purchases or licence activation
- With a MeshVault Server you configure
- When legally required

## Your Choices

You can use MeshVault without submitting feedback or enabling diagnostic mode. You may delete locally stored settings, logs and caches from your device.

To request access to or deletion of an identifiable feedback report, contact us and include enough information to locate the submission, such as its approximate date, title and contact address. Reports submitted without contact information may not be identifiable.
