MeshVault Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15-07-2026

MeshVault is a desktop application that manages 3D model files on your computer.

What data we collect

We do not collect personal information or telemetry by default.
The app stores your library metadata and settings locally on your device.
What data we store locally

File metadata (title, author, tags, source URL, thumbnails) for models you add.
App settings and preferences.
License status (if applicable).
How data is used

To display your library, search, and organize files.
To show model details and thumbnails.
To enable integrations (e.g., browser extension uploads or slicer launch).
Network access

The app may download thumbnails or fetch model resources when you provide a source URL.
If you use the MeshVault browser extension, the app accepts local uploads from it on your machine.
Data sharing

We do not sell or share your data.
No data is sent to third‑party analytics services.
Your control

All library data is stored locally and can be deleted by removing your library folder.
You can remove settings by deleting the app’s local settings files.
