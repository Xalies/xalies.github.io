Desktop Hub

Your Local Command Center

Built for large libraries and fast workflows. The Hub sits quietly on your network, organising your files and ready to receive models from either the Chrome extension or your phone.

  • Sorting options: Keep your library organised in folders, with collections (virtual folders) and support for file tags
  • Native Previews: View STL, OBJ, STEP, and G-Code files instantly without opening heavy CAD software.
  • Seamless Sync: Mirrors your phone's library with folder structures preserved.
  • Send to Slicer: Open models instantly in Cura, PrusaSlicer, or Bambu Studio.
  • Chrome Extension: Browsing on PC? Use the extension to download files directly into your Vault.
