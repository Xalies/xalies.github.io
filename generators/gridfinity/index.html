Gridfinity bins & bases.

Make a bin and its matching baseplate. Both use the same 42 mm grid. Choose a part, set the dimensions, and download an STL ready for your slicer. Everything runs on your device.

Walls & mounting

Standard open-frame baseplate. Width and depth stay the same when you switch parts, so the base matches your bin footprint. Increase them for a larger layout.

Grid pitch: 42 × 42 mm. Actual bin width and depth include fitting clearance. Compartments divide along the width only.
