02 / Five parts, one object

Print. Fit. Assemble.

  1. Print flat. Two frames, two rails and one deck. Start with 0.2 mm layers and 3 perimeters; inspect the slicer preview.
  2. Fit the rails. Slide both rails through the left frame. Align the right frame and slide it onto both rails.
  3. Square the frame. Rails protrude about 2 mm beyond each outer face. Check that both frames stand parallel.
  4. Place the deck. Centre it on the flat frame tops. Dry-fit first; optionally glue the joints and deck with an adhesive suitable for your material.

If slots bind, remove burrs or increase clearance and reprint the frames. If loose, reduce clearance or secure the joints after alignment.
