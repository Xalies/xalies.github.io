MeshVault

More than a folder scanner.

MeshVault is built around two real collection paths: importing creator packs and existing folders, or capturing repository downloads with their page details attached. The same portable library can then stay local, include watched folders, or connect to a dedicated server.

01

Collect

Import files, folders and archives with choices for keeping a pack intact, virtually indexing its contents, splitting models, or extracting its folder structure. MeshVault Connect handles supported repository downloads plus Google Drive and Dropbox packs.

02

Understand

Search titles, creators, sources, tags and descriptions; use thumbnails, Smart Collections, favourites and duplicate filtering. Keep model notes with optional surface pins, attach PDF guides, or let an authorised MCP assistant browse and organise library metadata.

03

Inspect and make

Preview common mesh, CAD and G-code formats, switch multi-plate projects, measure against a bed reference, capture renders, open files in FDM, resin or CNC tools, and retain useful slicer-output metrics.

04

Expand

Move the portable library between supported desktop clients, add in-place watched folders, host a shared local-network library with Windows, Linux or Docker server builds, or use MeshVault Mobile on Android and iOS.

The honest version

Reasons for—and reasons to look elsewhere.

These are not verdicts. They are practical reasons one product may suit a particular library better than another. Open-source and self-hosted tools offer different ways to manage and share a library.

USD $19.99 · lifetime licence and updates

MeshVault

Best suited to makers whose collection mixes creator packs, repository downloads, CAD and slicer files—and who want a portable local library that can later extend to a server or mobile device.

Choose MeshVault when

  • You want repository downloads to arrive with useful page metadata and images.
  • You need deliberate choices for large creator packs and nested archives.
  • You want model notes, optional print queues and native clients that combine local and server libraries.

Consider another tool when

  • You need an open-source desktop app.
  • You need public publishing, federation or OIDC in a web-first system.
  • You need built-in semantic search, geometry-aware duplicate review or printer and filament stock management. MCP assistant access is a different capability.

Licence and update coverage: MeshVault licence keys are managed through Lemon Squeezy and are valid for life. They include lifetime updates, including future major versions. Your licence key will never be limited to a particular product version, so a new major release does not require another licence purchase.

Store editions use their own purchase and activation process. Check the listing for your platform’s regional price.

Meshory

A strong scan-in-place desktop organiser built for very large existing libraries, with automatic rendering, optional AI search, geometry matching and folder-pack duplicate review.

Choose Meshory when

  • Your main problem is scanning and rendering a very large folder or NAS library in place.
  • You want optional cloud or local AI tagging, with OpenRouter-backed semantic search.
  • Geometry and folder-pack duplicate review, broad slicer detection or the beta 3MF converter matter.

Consider another tool when

  • You want browser repository imports or choices for extracting creator packs.
  • You need G-code layer inspection or in-app moves between library folders; these are respectively in progress and planned.
  • You want a dedicated local server, native mobile app, or a lower one-time price.

* Licence and update coverage: the purchase includes continued use of the app and every v1 update. Meshory currently plans v2 for August–September 2027, with an optional upgrade estimated at USD $10–15. Timing and pricing are not final.

Buying closer to v2 can mean a much shorter window of included updates and fixes. The licence covers v1 updates, rather than a fresh year of updates from your purchase date. If ongoing updates and fixes move to v2, someone buying just before its release could receive only weeks of further v1 updates before paying separately to follow new updates and fixes in v2.

The version you own keeps working without upgrading. Original USD $29 founding licences include future versions, including v2, at no extra cost. Pricing and v2 plans · Purchase terms

Free · open source

Printventory

A free, open-source organiser with mesh and CAD previews, print history, printer maintenance and optional AI tagging. Its Linux and Docker options are labelled experimental.

Choose Printventory when

  • Free and open source is a firm requirement.
  • You want bundle previews, CAD viewing, print history, printer maintenance and parts tracking.
  • You want a browser-extension inbox, optional AI tools or server/PWA access; Docker is experimental.

Consider another tool when

  • You need interactive Fusion or ChiTuBox geometry; their embedded pictures are not 3D previews.
  • You need the guided creator-pack and archive-placement choices described for MeshVault.
  • You want a portable database inside the library or native clients combining local and remote models.

Free · open source · self-hosted

Manyfold

A strong choice for a web-first, multi-user model library, with interactive mesh/CAD viewing, access controls, external-service integrations, APIs, optional plugins, model relationships and federation.

Choose Manyfold when

  • You want a shared browser-based library hosted on your own server.
  • Multiple users, permissions, OIDC, public/private sharing or federation matter.
  • You want plugins, an open API, model/remix relationships or direct printer integrations.

Consider another tool when

  • You prefer native desktop clients and a library that moves with its own database.
  • You want repository capture from the browser into the native app.
  • You need desktop preview measurements, slicer-result capture or more guided creator-pack imports.

Free · donation-supported

Orynt3D

A free desktop organiser focused on configurable imports, inherited tags and boolean or saved searches. Its official site still lists the browser-based server as coming soon.

Choose Orynt3D when

  • You want a free native desktop app across Windows, macOS and Linux.
  • Custom import configurations, inheritable tags and boolean search fit your way of organising.
  • Your packs contain useful cover images, with manual viewer captures available when needed.

Consider another tool when

  • You want generated thumbnails across a wider range of mesh, CAD and G-code formats.
  • You need browser repository capture, a documented duplicate finder or slicer-result tracking.
  • You need a released browser server or native mobile library.

Modelist

A desktop organiser with smart collections, source enrichment, duplicate review, multi-colour/multi-plate 3MF previews and OpenSCAD thumbnails. Its documented phone workflow delivers links to the desktop inbox; mobile library browsing is not documented.

Choose Modelist when

  • You want an unlimited-time trial library of up to 100 files, or qualify for educational use.
  • Smart collections, fuzzy search and exact/heuristic/perceptual duplicate review are priorities.
  • You value OpenSCAD thumbnails, printer/material metadata or opt-in phone sharing.

Consider another tool when

  • You need CAD/project formats beyond its indexed STEP and SCAD files, or G-code layer inspection.
  • You prefer a browser-extension capture workflow to source enrichment and shared links.
  • You need a shared library server or a native phone app with its own browsable library.

Free · Windows

STLOrganizer

A free Windows workshop organiser with conditional folder templates, a print-planning board, spool tracking and Cults3D discovery. The public Windows release is labelled a demo/beta.

Choose STLOrganizer when

  • You use Windows and want a free print-planning workspace rather than only a library.
  • Node-based folder templates, bulk file moves and commercial-licence tracking matter.
  • A kanban print queue and filament spool inventory are central to your workflow.

Consider another tool when

  • You need macOS/Linux clients or a portable library across operating systems.
  • You need CAD/G-code formats or archive formats beyond ZIP/RAR.
  • You want general repository capture, server-hosted libraries or native mobile browsing and transfers.
