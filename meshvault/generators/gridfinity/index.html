01 / Your workspace, your layout

Dimensions
Features
Magnets & screws

Choose either or both. Mounting points sit at the four corners of each grid cell. Match magnet polarity to your baseplate before fitting.

Baseplate footprint

Each square is one 42 mm cell. Click squares to combine them into your own footprint. Cells must connect along an edge to make one piece. Width and depth set the editing area.

Baseplate mounting

Reinforced base: 6.5 × 2.4 mm magnet pockets, 3.5 mm screw clearance holes and 8.5 mm countersinks underneath. Both are included at each cell’s corners. Total height: 10.8 mm.

Wall & floor thickness

Width and depth stay the same when you switch parts, so the base matches your bin footprint. Choose the reinforced base to secure it with magnets or screws.

Grid pitch: 42 × 42 mm. Actual bin width and depth include fitting clearance. Compartments divide along the width only.
