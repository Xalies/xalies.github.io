    Dimensions
    Features
    Magnet pockets

    Magnet pockets sit at the four corners of each grid cell. Match magnet polarity to your baseplate before fitting.

    Bin retention & drawer fixing
    Mount dimensions & fit

    Four 6.5 × 2.4 mm magnet pockets per cell hold matching magnet-equipped bins to the baseplate, with an open centre. To fasten the baseplate to your drawer floor, switch the grid to Centre screw positions and select only the cells you need. Each selected cell gets a 3.5 mm through-hole with a 7 mm countersink on top and support ribs.

    Standard frame: 5 mm high. Magnets or screw mounts add 3.6 mm underneath the sockets. Use countersunk screws with a length suited to your drawer-floor thickness. Check screw-head fit and magnet polarity before assembly.

    Baseplate footprint

    Toggle cells to shape one connected baseplate. In screw mode, click included cells to choose drawer fixing points, marked S. Each cell is 42 mm.

    Wall & floor thickness

    Width and depth stay the same when you switch parts, so the base matches your bin footprint. Magnets hold removable bins to the baseplate. Selected centre screws fasten the baseplate to your drawer floor.

    Grid pitch: 42 × 42 mm. Actual bin width and depth include fitting clearance. Compartments divide along the width only.

      About plate exports
      Each numbered plate contains its arranged parts. Drawer layouts import as one MeshVault project with their editable layout attached. Manually assembled sets import plates as separate library items, with the original part settings attached. Plans follow selected baseplate cells, keeping clearance between parts. Check placement and printable area in your slicer. These are model layouts, not sliced printer projects.
