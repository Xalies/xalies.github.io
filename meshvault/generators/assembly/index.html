Configure each half separately. Both inserts lift out and share the same housing. Tool holes leave 5 mm between their edges.

Each accessory includes a rear clamp and needs one M3 × 16 mm countersunk screw and M3 nut. Mounting plates have two 4.5 mm holes, 32 mm apart. Accessories need at least 8 height units. Test the fit in the socket coupon first.
