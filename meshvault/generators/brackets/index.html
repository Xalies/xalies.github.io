01 / Cut to measure

Bracket dimensions · mm
45° chamfers · optional

Zero leaves the edges square. Outside chamfers cover the perimeter and side edges; hole chamfers ease both openings while preserving the specified bore diameter. On saddles, the outer chamfer bevels the profile end faces while retaining the circular seat. These are edge breaks, not screw-head countersinks.

Individual mounting faces

Click a mounting face in the preview, or choose one below. The highlighted face can have its own holes; the other faces stay unchanged.

Offsets move the holes along the mounting face or across its width, from their default positions. Positive and negative values move in opposite directions.

Shared hole settings

Straight through-holes on the centreline. End distance sets the hole centres from the ends of each usable mounting face when using two holes. Measure your actual screws.

Preparing your bracket…
Shown in its side-down print orientation · mm

Prototype hardware, without a tested load rating. Check fit and fastening before use. Keep it away from structural or safety-critical applications.

Print guidance

From dimensions to a finished part.

Print on the profile side shown in the preview with a calibrated profile. Start with 0.2 mm layers, 4 perimeters and 40% infill. Inspect the hole bridges and corner in your slicer, and check the printed fit before use.
