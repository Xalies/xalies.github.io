MeshVault Support

Get help with MeshVault, MeshVault Server, MeshVault Mobile or MeshVault Connect.

Activate your licence

After a Stripe purchase, look for Your MeshVault Pro licence from support@meshvault.app at the email address you used at checkout.

  1. Install the direct download for your computer.
  2. Copy the full licence key from your purchase email.
  3. While connected to the internet, open Options in MeshVault, find the licence settings and paste your key. Choose Unlock with licence key if shown.

For MeshVault Server, open Options → Licence in the server’s browser interface, paste your key and choose Unlock server.

If the email hasn’t arrived, check your spam folder. If you still need help, send your receipt number to support@meshvault.app.

Existing Lemon Squeezy licence keys still work.

How connected devices count

A Stripe licence can be activated on up to 10 devices. When you connect to a library hosted by an activated MeshVault app or Server, you use the host’s licence status and library permissions without using another activation slot. Activate the licence on another device when you want to unlock its own local libraries.

The Apple App Store edition uses its own purchase and activation.

Contact support

For questions, technical support, feedback or feature requests, email support@meshvault.app.

When requesting help, please include:

  • The MeshVault product and version you are using
  • Your operating system and version
  • What you were trying to do
  • Any error message you received
