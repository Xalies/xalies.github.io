Before you download

Will it work with your collection?

STL, 3MF, OBJ, STEP, G-code and creator archives are all part of the workflow. Check the desktop support below for the files you use.

Which files can I keep in the library?

These formats can be catalogued, searched and organised. Some open in their usual application rather than a MeshVault preview.

3DM, 3DS, 3MF, ABC, AMF, BGCODE, BLEND, BLEND1, CATPART, CATPRODUCT, CNC, DAE, DWFX, DWG, DXF, F3D, FBX, FCSTD, GLB, GLTF, GCODE, GCO, IAM, IFC, IGES, IGS, IPT, JT, NC, OBJ, OFF, PLY, SAT, SAB, SCAD, SKP, SLDASM, SLDPRT, STEP, STL, STP, USD, USDA, USDC, USDZ, VRML, WRL, X_B, X_T, X3D

Which files can I preview?

The desktop apps preview the following formats and supported .model archive entries. Indexing a file or displaying its thumbnail does not necessarily mean it has an interactive preview.

STL, OBJ, OFF, AMF, 3DM, 3MF, STEP, STP, IGES, IGS, X3D, DXF, GCODE, GCO, NC, CNC, GLB, GLTF, PLY, DAE, FBX

Project contents and platform support can affect what is displayed. Mobile and browser previews have their own limits.

Can I bring in ZIP files and creator packs?

Yes. Keep a pack together or organise its supported contents in your library. Attach reference PDFs and instructions, and use .mvpack to share model listings with their details and supporting files.

ZIP, RAR, 7Z, TAR, TAR.GZ, TGZ, TAR.BZ2, TBZ2

Read about .mvpack packages →

Can I use my existing slicer?

Choose your FDM, resin or CNC application in MeshVault. The following supported file types can be handed directly to a compatible configured application.

STL, 3MF, OBJ, AMF, STEP, STP, GCODE, GCO, BGCODE

FBX and PLY can be converted to STL for hand-off; conversion results can vary. Supported 3MF compatibility options are available for OrcaSlicer, Snapmaker Orca and PrusaSlicer.

Support varies by file contents, platform and release. Ask about your workflow →
